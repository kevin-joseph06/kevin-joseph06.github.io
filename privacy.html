Privacy policy: gmail-multi

Last updated 26 October 2026.

gmail-multi is a personal desktop tool used only by its author, Kevin Joseph J, on his own mailboxes.

  • What it accesses: messages, labels and drafts in the Gmail accounts its author authorises, through the Gmail API.
  • Where data goes: nowhere. Mail is read on the author's own computer. Access tokens are stored only on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the author or to any third party.
  • Sharing and sale: no data is shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Google API Services: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
